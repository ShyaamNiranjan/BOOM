Common questions

What is BOOM?

BOOM is a unified business operations platform that helps companies manage inventory, billing, suppliers, workflows, reporting, portals, and internal coordination in one self-hosted system.

Is BOOM an ERP system?

BOOM is best understood as a modern ERP alternative. It centralizes inventory, billing, suppliers, workflows, portals, and reporting without the complexity and cost of traditional ERP software.

Can BOOM replace Excel?

Yes. BOOM replaces Excel and disconnected systems when companies need shared records, live dashboards, permissions, alerts, workflow ownership, and reporting across inventory, suppliers, billing, and team coordination.

Does BOOM support large teams?

Yes. BOOM supports scaling teams with role-based permissions, shared dashboards, workflow ownership, customer and supplier portals, reporting, and alerts.

Is BOOM self-hosted?

Yes. BOOM runs on your own system or server. You own the deployment and control the data environment — no mandatory SaaS lock-in.
