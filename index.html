Common questions

What is BOOM?

BOOM (Business Operations & Optimization Management) by YNIIDI is self-hosted business operations software. It puts orders, inventory, purchases, customers, suppliers, payments, tasks, portals and profit analytics in one system your company owns, bought once with a one-time license.

Is BOOM an ERP system?

BOOM is an ERP alternative. It covers the daily operations mid-sized businesses actually run — order management, inventory, purchasing, supplier and customer dues, payments and reporting — without a multi-year enterprise ERP programme. It is not a global, multi-entity suite at SAP or Oracle scale.

How much does BOOM cost?

BOOM Lite starts from ₹2,00,000 and BOOM starts from ₹5,00,000, both as a one-time license. Final price depends on customization scope and deployment. There are no per-seat fees and no mandatory SaaS subscription. See one-time license pricing.

Can BOOM replace Excel and WhatsApp for orders and inventory?

Yes. BOOM replaces Excel inventory sheets and WhatsApp order threads with shared records: every order captured in one place, live stock with low-stock alerts, and supplier and customer dues tracked in the system. Existing spreadsheet data is imported during setup.

Is BOOM self-hosted?

Yes. BOOM runs on infrastructure you control — on-premise, a VPS or a private cloud. The database, backups and access policies stay with you, and you keep running BOOM even if you never speak to YNIIDI again.

Which businesses is BOOM built for?

Wholesalers, distributors, retailers, manufacturers and import/export businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets and chat. Pricing is in INR for India, and BOOM works for operations-heavy businesses anywhere.

Does BOOM support larger teams?

Yes. BOOM has six built-in roles (Admin, Manager, Staff, Viewer, Customer, Supplier) and 40+ permission keys, so each person sees only what they should. Because there is no per-seat pricing, adding staff doesn’t add a monthly bill.

  • You’ve priced traditional ERP and found it too rigid, too expensive, or too slow to deploy
