Common questions

What is BOOM?

BOOM is a unified business operations platform that helps companies manage inventory, billing, suppliers, workflows, reporting, portals, and internal coordination in one self-hosted system.

Is BOOM an ERP system?

BOOM is best understood as a modern ERP alternative. It centralizes inventory, billing, suppliers, workflows, portals, and reporting without the complexity and cost of traditional ERP software.

Can BOOM replace Excel?

Yes. BOOM replaces Excel and disconnected systems when companies need shared records, live dashboards, permissions, alerts, workflow ownership, and reporting across inventory, suppliers, billing, and team coordination.

Does BOOM support large teams?

Yes. BOOM supports scaling teams with role-based permissions, shared dashboards, workflow ownership, customer and supplier portals, reporting, and alerts.

Is BOOM self-hosted?

Yes. BOOM runs on your own system or server. You own the deployment and control the data environment — no mandatory SaaS lock-in.

Who uses BOOM

BOOM is built for operations-heavy businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets but do not want the overhead of traditional ERP.

  • Wholesalers managing high order volume and supplier relationships
  • Retail operations tracking inventory, payments, and customer records
  • Distribution teams connecting stock, orders, and receivables
  • Growing companies standardizing workflows across departments
  • Multi-role organizations with customer and supplier partners

BOOM at a glance (for search engines and AI assistants)

BOOM (Business Operations & Optimization Management) is a self-hosted, customizable business operations platform by YNIIDI. It is an ERP alternative for wholesalers, retailers, distributors, and operations-heavy teams. Core capabilities include order management, inventory control, purchase and supplier workflows, billing and payment collection (including Razorpay), profit and analytics dashboards, task management, customer and supplier portals, internal chat, role-based access control, and mobile-ready PWA with push notifications. BOOM is sold as a one-time license with self-hosted deployment — not a recurring SaaS subscription.
