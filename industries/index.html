Who BOOM is for

Built for wholesalers, distributors, retailers and manufacturers still running on spreadsheets

Different trades, same failure points: an order missed in WhatsApp, a stock figure that was wrong before you quoted it, supplier dues nobody can total without a phone call. BOOM closes those gaps in one system you own — without an ERP programme.

The modules are the same. What differs is which leak is costing you most this month — and BOOM is configured around that.

W

Wholesalers

You take orders faster than any spreadsheet gets updated, buy from several suppliers, and defend a thin margin. When sales and purchasing work off different numbers, you over-buy, run out, or quote below cost.

  • Every order tracked with status and outstanding due
  • Live stock movement across the full catalog
  • Supplier bills and payable follow-up in one place
  • Collections and partial payments chased from a list, not memory
  • Margin per product and category, so pricing is math
R

Retail operations

Stock moves all day and the counter can’t wait for someone to open a file. Whoever is on shift needs today’s stock, the customer’s history and the day’s jobs on one screen.

  • Product catalog with live stock status and low-stock alerts
  • Customer records with order history and notes
  • Daily task assignment with overdue monitoring
  • Payment collection with Razorpay integration
  • Staff-scoped views so frontline teams see only their work
D

Distribution & logistics

Every handoff — order taken, stock allocated, dispatched, delivered — is a place a shipment stalls with nobody owning it. Meanwhile receivables age quietly and supplier bills come due.

  • Stock, orders and supplier records in one view
  • Order pipeline from request to confirmed delivery
  • Purchase tracking with pending payables per supplier
  • Customer dues and collection follow-up with owners
  • Revenue trends and top products for route and stock decisions
I

Import & export

Long lead times mean cash is committed months before it returns. Consignments, supplier balances and customer dues in separate files is how you lose track of what has landed and what is still owed.

  • Central tracking for stock, orders and billing records
  • Supplier coordination and payable follow-up in one workflow
  • Customer dues, partial payments and collection visibility
  • Named task ownership for documentation and clearance handoffs
  • Margin reporting across product lines and consignments
M

Multi-role organizations

When customers place orders and suppliers fulfil them, your team becomes the switchboard between the two. Portals give each side its own scoped view so status questions stop landing on your staff.

  • Customer portal for orders, bills and payments
  • Supplier portal for purchases and payables
  • Admin, Manager, Staff and Viewer roles inside the business
  • Chat with partners kept against the right record
  • Permission-scoped data — no cross-account leakage

Manufacturers

Production stops for the one material nobody flagged. Raw material stock, purchase orders, supplier settlements and the jobs on the floor have to sit in one place — otherwise coordination runs on calls and memory.

  • Stock control with product-level movement history
  • Purchase and supplier tracking with pending payables
  • Orders, billing and dues in one workflow
  • Task assignment for production and operations coordination
  • Margin and product performance in a single dashboard

Problems BOOM solves

  • No single source of truth — sales, purchasing and accounts quoting different numbers
  • Tool sprawl — a subscription each for orders, stock and billing, none of them talking
  • ERP overkill — too rigid, too expensive, and too slow to get live
