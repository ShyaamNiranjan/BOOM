Industry questions

Is BOOM good inventory management software for wholesalers?

BOOM is built for wholesale operations: every order tracked with status and outstanding dues, live stock across the catalog, supplier bills and payables in one place, and margin per product so pricing is based on real numbers. It is self-hosted and sold as a one-time license.

Can distributors track customer receivables and supplier payables in BOOM?

Yes. BOOM records customer dues and partial payments against orders, and supplier bills and pending payables against purchases, so both sides of the ledger can be totalled without phone calls.

Does BOOM work for manufacturers?

Yes. BOOM started with a manufacturing business running on Excel and WhatsApp. Manufacturers use it for raw material and product stock, purchase and supplier tracking, orders and dues, task assignment for production coordination, and margin reporting. Read the tissue paper manufacturer case study.

Is BOOM a fit for very small businesses?

Usually not. BOOM is a one-time investment starting from ₹2,00,000, so it suits operations-heavy businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets. Very small or solo operations, or buyers wanting a cheap monthly SaaS tool, are better served elsewhere.
