Who BOOM is for

BOOM supports real operational complexity

Whether you are a wholesaler processing hundreds of orders a week, a retailer managing stock across locations, or a growing team drowning in spreadsheets — BOOM gives you one system to run inventory, billing, suppliers, and workflows without the overhead of traditional ERP.

Industry use cases

BOOM is not limited to one vertical. It adapts to how your business operates — with customizable modules, permissions, and workflows.

W

Wholesalers & distributors

High order volume, multiple suppliers, and tight margin control demand a system that connects purchasing to sales in real time.

  • Order throughput tracking with status and due management
  • Stock movement visibility across product catalog
  • Supplier bill tracking and payable follow-up
  • Customer collection and partial payment workflows
  • Profit margin analysis per product and category
R

Retail operations

Retail teams need fast stock updates, customer records, and daily workflow coordination — without enterprise software complexity.

  • Product catalog with stock status and low-stock alerts
  • Customer records with order history and notes
  • Daily task assignment and overdue monitoring
  • Payment collection with Razorpay integration
  • Staff-scoped views for frontline team members
D

Distribution & logistics

Distribution teams juggle inventory, order fulfillment, supplier relationships, and receivables — often across disconnected tools.

  • Unified view of stock, orders, and supplier records
  • Order status pipeline from request to delivery
  • Supplier purchase tracking and pending payables
  • Customer dues and collection follow-up
  • Analytics for revenue trends and top products
G

Growing companies

When a business outgrows spreadsheets, the pain shows up as duplicate data entry, missed follow-ups, and managers flying blind.

  • Role-based permissions as team size increases
  • Shared dashboards replacing manual reporting
  • Workflow ownership with task assignments
  • Start with core modules, expand scope over time
  • One system instead of five disconnected apps
M

Multi-role organizations

Businesses with external partners — customers who place orders and suppliers who fulfill purchases — need controlled portal access.

  • Customer portal for orders, bills, and payments
  • Supplier portal for purchases and payables
  • Admin, Manager, Staff, and Viewer internal roles
  • Chat between admin and external partners
  • Permission-scoped data — no cross-account leakage
S

Operations-first startups

Startups that sell physical products or manage supplier relationships need operational infrastructure early — not after chaos sets in.

  • Deploy core modules from day one
  • Scale permissions and workflows as team grows
  • Self-hosted — no per-seat SaaS costs eating margins
  • Customizable to match evolving business model
  • Profit visibility from the first sales cycle

Problems BOOM solves

Most operations teams hit the same wall: data lives in too many places, follow-ups get missed, and managers cannot see what is actually happening until it is too late.

  • Spreadsheet chaos — inventory, orders, and supplier data in separate files with no live connection
  • Missed follow-ups — due payments, overdue tasks, and low-stock alerts buried in chat threads
  • No single source of truth — sales, purchasing, and finance teams working from different numbers
  • Tool sprawl — one app for orders, another for inventory, another for billing, none talking to each other
  • ERP overkill — traditional ERP is too complex, too expensive, and too slow to adopt for mid-size operations
