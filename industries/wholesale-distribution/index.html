Where wholesale operations leak

None of these is one dramatic failure. Each is a small loss that repeats every week.

01

Order volume outruns the sheet

Orders arrive on WhatsApp, calls and from salesmen in the field. By the time they reach a spreadsheet, one has been missed, one has been billed twice, and nobody is sure which went out.

02

Party-wise dues live in memory

Credit is how wholesale sells. When outstanding per party is worked out only at month-end, you keep supplying the retailer who is already sixty days behind.

03

Supplier payables drift

Several suppliers, part payments, bills that don’t match the goods received. “What do we owe them?” becomes a phone call instead of a lookup.

04

Stock across godowns

The sales team promises stock the godown doesn’t have, or the godown sits on stock sales doesn’t know about. Either way, someone quotes from yesterday’s number.

05

Retailers calling for status

“Has my order gone?” “What’s my balance?” Every one of those calls pulls a staff member off billing or dispatch.

06

Thin margins, guessed

On a thin wholesale margin, one wrongly priced product line wipes out the profit on several others — and without cost next to revenue, you won’t see which one.

How BOOM handles each one

Real screens from BOOM. Workflows are adapted to how your business runs during scoping.

Orders: one record from placement to payment

Every order — whether it came from WhatsApp, a call or a salesman — gets one record with its status, invoice number and what is still due on it. Nothing depends on scrolling back through a chat.

  • Status tracking from pending through delivered or cancelled
  • Invoice numbers, due amounts and partial payments per order
  • Bulk status updates for busy dispatch days
  • Order import from spreadsheets during migration

Party-wise customer dues

Each customer record carries their order history, what they’ve paid and what they still owe. Before the next consignment goes out, anyone with access can see where that party stands.

  • Outstanding and payment status per customer
  • Partial payments recorded against the orders they settle
  • Customer segments and revenue per party
  • Collections chased from a list, not from memory

Live stock and low-stock alerts

Stock moves as orders and purchases move, so sales quotes from the same number the godown sees. Low stock shows up on the dashboard while there’s still time to reorder.

  • SKU-level tracking with categories and supplier mapping
  • Low-stock and out-of-stock alerts
  • Stock updates with reason logging
  • Multiple godowns: how locations are represented is agreed during scoping

Supplier payables and payments

Purchases, supplier bills and pending amounts sit against each supplier, linked to the stock they brought in. Receivables and payables are visible in one place, each traced back to the order or purchase behind it.

  • Supplier bills, pending amounts and payout history
  • Purchase orders linked to the product catalog
  • Customer collections with Razorpay checkout
  • Transaction references and payment history filters

A portal for your retailers

In the full BOOM edition, each retailer or dealer gets a login to see their own orders, bills, pending dues and payment history — and pay from the same screen. They see their account and nothing else.

  • Order status and billing visible to the retailer
  • Pending dues and payment history per account
  • Online payment via Razorpay
  • Chat with your team kept against the account

Margin per product

Revenue, cost of goods and margin are calculated from the orders and purchases already in BOOM. See which lines earn, which ones don’t, and price accordingly.

  • Revenue, COGS, profit and margin over time
  • Top-performing and loss-making products
  • 7-day, 30-day and custom date ranges

Sales, godown, purchase and accounts all work the same records, each seeing what their role allows. Six roles — Admin, Manager, Staff, Viewer, Customer, Supplier — and 40+ permission keys decide who can open what.

Wholesale and distribution questions

Is BOOM an ERP for wholesalers in India?

BOOM is a self-hosted operations platform built for wholesalers and distributors: orders and billing, live stock with low-stock alerts, purchases, party-wise customer dues, supplier payables, payments and profit per product. It is sold as a one-time license and customized to your workflow during scoping.

Can BOOM show party-wise outstanding?

Yes. Customer dues and partial payments are recorded against each order, so what each party owes you is visible per customer. Supplier bills and pending payables are recorded the same way against each supplier.

Can our retailers check their own orders and dues?

Yes, in the full BOOM edition. The customer portal lets each retailer see their own orders, bills, pending dues and payment history, and pay through Razorpay, without seeing any other account.

Does BOOM handle stock across multiple godowns?

How your godowns are represented is agreed during scoping, because every distributor splits stock differently. Tell us how many locations you run and how stock moves between them, and we will show you how BOOM maps to it before you commit.

Does BOOM replace our GST billing or accounting software?

No. BOOM runs the operation: orders, stock, purchases, dues, payments and profit. It does not file GST returns or generate e-invoices or e-way bills, so keep your accounting software for statutory work.
