BOOM for manufacturers, in one paragraph

BOOM by YNIIDI is self-hosted order and inventory software for small and mid-sized Indian manufacturers. It tracks customer orders and billing, raw material and finished goods stock with low-stock alerts, purchases and supplier dues, customer dues and payments, production coordination tasks with owners and deadlines, and profit per product. It is customized to each plant’s workflow and sold as a one-time license. BOOM’s first deployment was a tissue paper manufacturer. It does not include packaged BOM, MRP or production planning modules, and it is not a GST filing system.

What a small plant actually has to keep track of

Not an enterprise planning suite. The daily questions the owner, the floor supervisor and accounts ask each other.

01

Which orders are due, and for whom?

Buyer orders with quantities, status and dispatch dates, all in one list instead of scattered across chats and calls.

02

Do we have the material?

Raw material stock that is current, with a warning before it runs low — not when the line is already waiting.

03

What is ready to ship?

Finished goods stock alongside raw material, so sales can commit without walking to the godown to count.

04

What do we owe suppliers?

Material purchases, supplier bills and part payments recorded against each supplier, so payables are a lookup.

05

Who owes us, and how much?

Customer dues and partial payments tracked against each order, so collections are chased from a list.

06

Are we actually making money on it?

Revenue, cost and margin per product, calculated from the orders and purchases already recorded.

How BOOM covers it

Real screens from BOOM. During scoping we adapt the workflows to your plant rather than asking you to change how you operate.

Raw material and finished goods stock

Keep raw materials and finished goods as separate categories in one catalog. Stock moves as purchases come in and orders go out, and every manual adjustment carries a logged reason.

  • SKU-level stock with categories and supplier mapping
  • Low-stock and out-of-stock alerts on the dashboard
  • Quick stock updates with reason logging
  • Stock history on each product

Customer orders and billing

Each buyer order gets one record from placement to payment — status, invoice number, due amount and notes — so nothing depends on who took the call.

  • Status tracking from pending to delivered
  • Invoice numbers, due amounts and partial payments
  • Customer-linked order history
  • Order import from spreadsheets during migration

Supplier and customer dues

What you owe material suppliers and what buyers owe you sit in one view, each traced to the purchase or order behind it.

  • Supplier bills, pending payables and payout history
  • Customer collections with partial payment support
  • Razorpay checkout for online collections
  • Supplier portal for bill and payment visibility in the full BOOM edition

Tasks for production coordination

“Follow up on the material delivery,” “dispatch the order by Thursday” — assigned in a chat, forgotten by Friday. In BOOM every task has an owner, a due date and a visible status.

  • Priority, due date and assignee on each task
  • Pending, in-progress, completed and overdue tracking
  • Comments and file attachments
  • Push notifications on the mobile-ready PWA

Profit visibility per product

Revenue, cost of goods and margin sit next to each other, so you can see which product lines carry the plant and which ones quietly lose money.

  • Revenue, COGS, profit and margin trends
  • Top-performing and loss-making products
  • Time-filtered dashboards and exports

Manufacturing questions

Is BOOM suitable for small manufacturers in India?

Yes, for the operational side of manufacturing: customer orders and billing, raw material and finished goods stock, purchases and supplier dues, customer dues, tasks for production coordination and profit visibility. BOOM’s first deployment was a tissue paper manufacturer.

Does BOOM include BOM, MRP or production planning?

These are not part of BOOM’s standard modules. BOOM covers orders, stock, purchases, supplier and customer dues, payments, tasks and profit. If your process needs something specific, raise it during scoping and we will tell you plainly what can be customized and what cannot.

Can BOOM track raw material and finished goods separately?

Yes. Raw materials and finished goods can be kept as separate categories in the same catalog, each with SKU-level stock, supplier mapping, low-stock alerts and stock updates with a logged reason.

Does BOOM handle GST filing for manufacturers?

No. BOOM does not file GST returns or generate e-invoices or e-way bills, and it does not replace statutory accounting software. It runs the operation alongside your accounting system.

What does BOOM cost for a manufacturer?

In India, BOOM Lite starts from ₹2,00,000 and BOOM starts from ₹5,00,000, both one-time licenses with no per-seat fees. Final price depends on the customization your workflow needs.
