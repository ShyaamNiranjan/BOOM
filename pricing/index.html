Pricing FAQ

Is there a monthly subscription?

No. BOOM is a one-time license purchase. After purchase, you own the system forever. There is no mandatory recurring monthly or annual subscription fee.

How is the final price decided?

Pricing depends on selected modules, customization depth, user roles, portal requirements, deployment architecture, and implementation scope. We discuss requirements first, then provide a scoped estimate.

Can we start with fewer modules?

Yes. Many clients begin with orders, inventory, and billing — then add suppliers, analytics, portals, and advanced workflows in later phases.

Does pricing include portal customization?

Customer and supplier portal flows are included in scope planning. Custom portal features are priced as part of the customization scope.

Are there hidden costs?

No hidden license fees. Your costs are the one-time license, agreed customization scope, and deployment setup. Server hosting is on your infrastructure.

How does BOOM compare to SaaS pricing?

SaaS tools charge per user per month forever. BOOM charges once — so as your team grows, your software cost does not scale with headcount.
