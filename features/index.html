What BOOM replaces

Running operations across chat and spreadsheets has a price. These modules are how you stop paying it.

Orders in WhatsApp, stock in a file that was accurate yesterday, supplier dues in someone’s head — every gap between those is where an order goes missing, a stockout goes unnoticed, or a payment goes unchased. BOOM puts the same records in front of everyone who needs them, on your servers, for one payment.

Orders, stock, purchases, payments, tasks and profit all read from the same customers, products and suppliers. Nothing gets re-entered, and nobody has to ask another team what the current number is.

Stop being the status desk for your own customers

Your team answers the same three questions all day: where is my order, what do I owe, when were we paid. Portals let customers and suppliers answer those themselves — scoped so each one sees only their own account.

Customer portal

Every “where is my order?” call pulls someone off actual work. Customers see their own orders, bills, dues and payment history — and can pay from the same screen.

  • View order status and billing details
  • See pending dues and payment history
  • Pay via Razorpay checkout from the portal
  • Chat with admin for support and requests

Supplier portal

Suppliers chasing payment status make your team reconcile the same bill twice. They get a restricted view of their purchases, bills, pending amount and recent payments — and nothing else.

  • Purchase and bill visibility scoped to their account
  • Pending amount totals and payment history
  • Direct chat with admin for queries
  • No access to other suppliers' or internal data

Role-based access control

Who can see what shouldn’t depend on trust and memory. Six roles — Admin, Manager, Staff, Viewer, Customer, Supplier — decide what each person can open, and the navigation adapts so nobody is browsing screens they aren’t meant to see.

  • 40+ granular permission keys across all modules
  • Permission-scoped navigation — no unauthorized menu items
  • Route-level guards enforce access on every page
  • Custom role configuration during implementation

Chat & notifications

A decision made in a personal chat leaves no trace against the order it changed. Conversations stay inside BOOM, attached to the customer, supplier or task they’re about.

  • Admin-to-customer and admin-to-supplier chat threads
  • Search, unread counts, and seen status
  • File attachments in conversation threads
  • Push notifications for tasks, payments, chat, and low-stock alerts

What is BOOM?

BOOM by YNIIDI (Business Operations & Optimization Management) is a self-hosted ERP alternative sold as a one-time license. It replaces Excel inventory sheets, WhatsApp order threads and supplier dues kept from memory with one customizable system for order management and billing, inventory management, purchase and supplier management, customer and supplier dues, payment collection, customer and supplier portals, profit reporting, task management and role-based team coordination. It runs on the customer’s own servers, with no per-seat fees and no mandatory SaaS subscription. See one-time license pricing or how wholesalers, distributors and manufacturers use it.

Module questions

Does BOOM include order management?

Yes. Every order gets one record from placement to payment, with status tracking, invoice numbers, due amounts, partial payments, bulk status updates and order import from spreadsheets. It replaces taking and tracking orders in WhatsApp threads.

Can BOOM replace an Excel inventory sheet?

Yes. BOOM’s inventory management tracks stock at SKU level with categories and supplier mapping. Stock moves as orders and purchases move, and low-stock alerts appear on the dashboard before you run out.

How does BOOM track supplier dues?

Purchases, supplier bills and pending payables are recorded against each supplier and linked to the stock they supplied, so what you owe each supplier is a lookup rather than a phone call.

Which BOOM edition includes the customer and supplier portals?

The full BOOM edition (from ₹5,00,000, one-time license) includes the customer portal, supplier portal and payment gateway integration. BOOM Lite (from ₹2,00,000) covers the core internal modules without portals.
