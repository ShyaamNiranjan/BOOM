Feature Deep Dive

Core BOOM modules for operational control

BOOM V2 includes the modules most operations teams need in one place, with shared data and role-based access.

Orders

Create, assign, and track order progress with billing status and due tracking.

Inventory

Track stock, low-stock alerts, SKU-level changes, and product performance.

Purchases & suppliers

Manage purchase records, supplier payables, payment history, and follow-ups.

Payments

Track customer collections, supplier payouts, partial payments, and references.

Tasks & workflows

Run work pipelines with due dates, priorities, status updates, and ownership.

Analytics & profit

Monitor revenue trends, COGS, margin, top products, and loss-making items.

Portals and access controls

  • Customer portal for bills, dues, and payment history
  • Supplier portal for purchase and payable visibility
  • RBAC with Admin, Manager, Staff, Viewer, Customer, Supplier roles
  • Permission-scoped navigation and route controls
