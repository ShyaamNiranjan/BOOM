Self-hosted ERP alternative · India

A self-hosted ERP alternative: own the system your operations run on.

Once orders, stock and dues outgrow Excel and WhatsApp, you have four realistic options. Rent a SaaS ERP. Implement an open-source ERP yourself. Buy desktop billing software. Or have a system customized to your operation and installed on your own server. Here is how they actually compare — and what self-hosting asks of you in return.

What is a self-hosted ERP alternative?

A self-hosted ERP alternative is business operations software installed on a server you control — on-premise, a VPS or a private cloud — instead of the vendor’s cloud, so the database, backups and access rules stay with you. BOOM by YNIIDI is one: a customized, vendor-implemented platform for orders and billing, inventory, purchases, supplier and customer dues, payments, portals and profit visibility, sold as a one-time license with no per-seat fees. It is not a GST filing or statutory accounting replacement.

Who each option actually fits

None of these is wrong. Each is wrong for somebody.

SaaS ERP or cloud billing app

Fits small teams who want to start this week, don’t want any server responsibility, and are comfortable adapting their process to the product. The trade-off is a bill that recurs for as long as you use it and usually grows with users, and data that sits on someone else’s infrastructure.

Open-source ERP you implement yourself

Fits businesses with in-house developers or a trusted implementation partner, and the patience for a longer project. The licence may cost nothing; the implementation, customization and ongoing upkeep do. If the person who configured it leaves, so does the knowledge.

Desktop one-time-license billing software

Fits businesses whose main need is invoicing, GST and books on one or a few machines. It is usually the right tool for accounting. It is usually not built for a sales team, godown staff, purchase and accounts all working the same orders from different places.

BOOM

Fits operations-heavy wholesalers, distributors, manufacturers and traders who have outgrown Excel and WhatsApp, want the system shaped around how they already work, and would rather pay once and own it. You keep your accounting software for statutory work; BOOM runs the operation around it.

Why self-host at all

Renting your operations means someone else holds the switch. A price change, a plan retired, an account suspended over a billing dispute — and your order history is on the other side of it. Self-hosting removes that dependency.

  • Your database, on infrastructure you choose and control
  • Backup policies you define, and exports you can take any day
  • No per-seat meter, so adding godown or sales staff adds no monthly bill
  • Access rules enforced by your own role and permission setup
  • You keep running BOOM even if you never speak to YNIIDI again

What self-hosting requires from you

Owning the system means owning a few responsibilities. They are not heavy, but they are real, and it is better to plan for them before go-live than after a disk fails.

  • A server. An on-premise machine, a VPS or a private cloud instance, on Linux or Windows Server with a standard web stack. We help plan the architecture during scoping.
  • Backups that are tested. BOOM includes backup, restore and data export. Someone still has to schedule backups, keep a copy off the server, and occasionally check that a restore works.
  • Someone responsible. One named person or your local IT vendor who owns server access, HTTPS, user accounts and restores. It does not need to be a full-time role.

Self-hosted ERP questions

What is a self-hosted ERP alternative?

It is business operations software installed on a server you control, on-premise, a VPS or a private cloud, instead of the vendor’s cloud. Your database, backups and access rules stay with you. BOOM by YNIIDI is one: a customized operations platform for orders, inventory, purchases, dues, payments and portals, sold as a one-time license.

Is BOOM open source?

No. BOOM is commercial software sold as a one-time license and implemented by YNIIDI. You get a system customized to your workflows and set up on your server, rather than a free codebase you implement yourself.

What do we need in-house to self-host BOOM?

A server (on-premise, VPS or private cloud, Linux or Windows Server), a backup routine that someone actually checks, and one named person or IT vendor responsible for the server, access and restores. YNIIDI handles installation, data migration and team enablement.

Does BOOM handle GST returns and statutory accounting?

No. BOOM runs operations: orders, billing, stock, purchases, supplier and customer dues, payments and profit visibility. It does not file GST returns or replace your statutory accounting software, which most businesses keep alongside it.

Who should not choose BOOM?

Businesses looking for a cheap monthly tool, very small or solo operations, and enterprises needing SAP-scale multi-entity suites. BOOM fits operations-heavy businesses that have outgrown Excel and WhatsApp and want to own their system.
